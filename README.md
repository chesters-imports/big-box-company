**Big Box Company** makes **documentation industrial** software.  
Fluorescent lights. Beige carpet that remembers nothing. A break room that smells like toner and resignation. We do **not** kiss the wires. We **file**.

We are not Charlie’s Toys. We are not DATBOX Studio.  
We are the office that ships **Office / Confluence energy** — without the cloud, without the vibes, without the romance. Full **Daniel Wake, Product Developer**: product briefs, section bins, ship dates that slip by one quarter forever.
